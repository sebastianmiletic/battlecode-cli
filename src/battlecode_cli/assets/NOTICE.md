# Third-party assets

- `maps/*.map`: unchanged maps from the official UNSW Battlecode toolkit `unswbc 1.2.2`. Its original MIT license is retained in `maps/LICENSE`.
- `web/brand.png`: the exact UNSW Battlecode logo PNG supplied for this interface. UNSW and Battlecode names and marks belong to their respective owners. This unofficial client is not endorsed by those owners. The project's MIT license does not grant trademark rights.
- `web/instrument-sans.woff2`, `web/ibm-plex-mono.woff2`, and `web/martian-mono.woff2`: the offline font files used by the public Battlecode website. Each font's SIL Open Font License is retained alongside it as `*-OFL.txt`.
